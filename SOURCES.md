# Sources for updated-model risk assumptions

- [Updated Airbus Challenge 3 specification](../Autonomous%20SG%202026%20-%20Airbus%20Problem%20Statements%20%28Updated%29.pdf)
- [ICAO SAF Rules of Thumb](https://www.icao.int/SAF/saf-rules-of-thumb) — pathway yields, facility scales, capital cost, and minimum selling-price context.
- [DOE SAF Grand Challenge Roadmap](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2022-09/beto-saf-gc-roadmap-report-sept-2022.pdf) — regional feedstock, infrastructure, logistics, and risk-reduction modeling.
- [DOE SAF Implementation Framework](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-11/SAF-Implementation-Framework-Nov-2024.pdf) — feedstock logistics resilience and handling reliability.
- [DOE Feedstock Logistics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/fuels/feedstock-logistics) — collection, storage, quality, handling losses, and fire-risk considerations.
- [ICAO CORSIA SAF Feedstocks](https://www.icao.int/CORSIA/feedstocks) — sustainability categories and certification context.
- [Gaveau et al. (2013), Reconciling Forest Conservation and Logging in Indonesian Borneo](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0069887) — source for the protected-area/concession reference figure; historical context, not a current GIS exclusion mask.
